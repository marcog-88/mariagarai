# SOP-001 · Monday Weekly Ritual
**Project:** Exponencial
**Owner:** Marco
**Frequency:** Every Monday morning
**Time required:** 10 minutes max
**Version:** 1.1 · June 2026

---

## Purpose

Keep the sprint on track by doing a short weekly sync with Claude every Monday. This replaces long planning sessions and prevents scope creep. It is the single most important habit in the sprint.

---

## When to do this

Every Monday, before you start any build work. First thing.

---

## What you need

- This Claude project open (Exponencial)
- Your Notion sprint tracker open in another tab
- 10 minutes of uninterrupted time

---

## Steps

**Step 1 — Open this Claude project**
Always use the Exponencial project in Claude.ai, not a new conversation. The project instructions and memory mean Claude already knows the context.

**Step 2 — Paste the week's Notion checklist**
Go to your Notion sprint tracker. Find the current week's page. Copy all tasks exactly as they appear, including their status (done / in progress / blocked / not started). Paste them here into the conversation.

**Step 3 — Add a one-line status note**
After pasting the checklist, write one sentence about anything that feels stuck or has changed since last week. Example: "María hasn't filmed yet, waiting on mic setup." or "Everything on track, no blockers."

**Step 4 — Claude reviews and responds**
Claude will output:
- What got done (acknowledged)
- What's blocked and why it matters
- Top 3 priorities for Marco this week
- Top 3 priorities for María this week (if applicable)
- Any scope adjustments needed

**Step 5 — Update Notion**
Take Claude's reprioritized list and update your Notion tracker for the week. This becomes your working list until next Monday.

---

## What this is NOT

- Not a long strategy session
- Not a place to add new ideas to the sprint (write those in a separate Notion page called "Backlog")
- Not optional — if you skip Monday, the sprint drifts

---

## Example prompt to paste every Monday

```
Monday check-in. Here's this week's Notion checklist:

[paste tasks here]

Status note: [one sentence on blockers or changes]
```

---

## Notes

- If something major changes mid-week (María gets sick, a tool breaks, a client emergency), open Claude and do a mini check-in. Don't wait until Monday.
- This SOP itself should be updated if the ritual format changes. Version it (v1.0, v1.1, etc.) and note what changed and why.
- Future versions of this SOP will be the basis for the productized system we sell to other operators.
